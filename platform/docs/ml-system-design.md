# ML System Design

## Prediction Tasks

| Task | Model family | Serving mode | Primary output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burnout prediction | XGBoost / calibrated ensemble | Online + batch | 0-1 probability over 24h/7d/14d horizons |
| Fatigue forecasting | LightGBM quantile regression + temporal model | Online + batch | P50/P90 fatigue trajectory |
| Staffing optimization | constrained optimizer + learned demand forecast | Nearline | staffing deltas by department/shift |
| Intervention recommendation | rules + uplift model, later contextual bandit | Online with human approval | ranked intervention actions |
| Anomaly detection | Isolation Forest + robust cohort residuals | Online | anomaly score and anomaly type |
| Operational risk scoring | ensemble aggregator | Online + executive batch | department/service-line risk index |

## Labels

Synthetic mode labels are generated by the simulator and are useful for validating plumbing and model behavior. Production labels must be governance-approved and should avoid punitive individual monitoring.

Recommended labels:

- `burnout_label_14d`: staff member crosses high-risk state, requests fatigue support, hits approved operational fatigue proxy, or has repeated critical predictions reviewed by coordinator.
- `fatigue_target_24h`: observed fatigue index 24h after feature cutoff.
- `workload_target_24h`: observed patient pressure and staffing ratio composite in next 24h.
- `intervention_success_7d`: accepted intervention reduces risk by a minimum policy threshold without creating downstream department overload.

## Feature Windows

- 5 minute windows: online volatility, alarms, acute stress.
- 1 hour windows: operational risk and gateway inference.
- 24 hour windows: recovery, sleep debt, anomaly counts.
- 7/14/30 day windows: overtime, night shift rotation, absence pressure, intervention history.

## Leakage Prevention

- Every training row has `feature_cutoff_at`.
- Labels must be observed strictly after `feature_cutoff_at`.
- Do not use post-intervention outcomes as pre-intervention features unless the feature window is after the intervention.
- Separate staff-level longitudinal splits from random row splits for final validation.
- Backtest by date to capture seasonality and staffing-policy changes.

## Evaluation

Burnout model:

- AUROC for ranking quality.
- AUPRC because high-risk events are rare.
- Brier score and calibration slope for probability quality.
- Recall at operational threshold, measured with alert volume.
- Lead time before escalation.

Fatigue/workload forecast:

- MAE/RMSE.
- Pinball loss for quantiles.
- Prediction interval coverage.
- Department-level residual monitoring.

Recommendation engine:

- acceptance rate.
- observed risk reduction.
- downstream overload penalty.
- fairness and policy review by role/department/shift.

## Explainability

- Use SHAP for XGBoost/LightGBM offline and cache top feature attributions with predictions.
- Online service returns top drivers in the prediction response.
- Frontend should show driver direction: increasing risk, decreasing risk, or protective.
- Keep explanation language operational: "sleep debt and alarm exposure are driving risk", not diagnostic claims.

## Calibration

- Calibrate probabilities with isotonic regression or Platt scaling on a holdout set.
- Track calibration separately by department, role, shift type, and day/night rotation.
- Do not promote a model if high-risk probabilities are poorly calibrated, even if AUROC improves.
